Most clips lose the best part of the conversation. They give you the setup, but cut away before the idea lands. A better clip contains one complete thought: a clear opening, a useful payoff, and a natural place to stop. That is why we keep the original voice, choose a continuous excerpt, and check the finished video. Captions should help you follow the idea, without covering the person who is speaking. You can change the headline, choose your own colours, and review every moment before you share it. The goal is simple. Give a good conversation another chance to be heard.

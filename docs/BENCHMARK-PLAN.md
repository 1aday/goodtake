# Prove the result before claiming superiority

No AutoClip comparison has been completed for this release. The initial code/demo review justified
a prototype, not a general ranking of either product's current output.

Use ten rights-cleared conversations spanning solo footage, split screens, camera changes, panels,
accents and imperfect audio. Give both products the same source and declared output count. Record
release/commit, settings, hardware and provider choices. Separate default and tuned runs. Include
every attempted source and failure.

Have reviewers who cannot see tool names assess complete opening/payoff/ending, truthful headlines,
caption errors, framing and willingness to publish. Report disagreement. The useful primary measure
is usable clips per hour of operator effort.

Run a second comparison on identical fixed excerpts to isolate typography, timing, framing and
encoding from moment selection. Preserve all variants rather than selecting only our best examples.

Record installation effort, wall time, failures, human correction time and cost by component. Unknown
compute/provider charges stay unknown. Do not compare cached render-only time with another tool's
full download/transcription/editorial pipeline. Publish permissions, commands, all outputs and rubrics.
